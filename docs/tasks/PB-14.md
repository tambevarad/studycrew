# PB-14 Group polls

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**User story:** As a member, I can create a poll, vote, and see the final choice recorded.

## Files you own

`src/server/services/polls/**`, `src/app/api/v1/teams/[teamId]/polls/**`, `src/app/(app)/teams/[teamId]/polls/**`,
`src/components/features/polls/**`, tests `polls*`.

## Build

- Service: `createPoll` (2–6 unique options, `closesAt` must be in the future → 400; `POLL_CREATED` activity + notify
  others), `vote` (upsert one vote per member; 409 "This poll has closed." after `closesAt`/`closedAt`), `closePoll`
  (creator or leader; early close), `closeDuePolls(teamId, now)` called by list/get to lazily close expired polls,
  setting `closedAt`, `winningOptionId` (most votes; tie → lowest `position`; no votes → null) and logging `POLL_CLOSED`
  with summary "closed the poll “Q” — winner: “A” (3 votes)" + notify `POLL_CLOSED`. `listPolls` (open first, then closed).
- Pure `results.ts`: `tally(options, votes)`, `pickWinner(tally)`.
- Page: open polls with radio options (change vote until close), live counts after voting, closing countdown;
  closed polls show the winner highlighted (`success`) and a bar per option with counts as text. Create dialog with
  dynamic option list (add/remove, 2–6), close date/time.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: A poll needs 2–6 distinct options and a future closing time.
- AC2: Each member has one vote that can change until the poll closes; after closing voting returns 409.
- AC3: When a poll closes (time passed or closed early), the final choice is recorded in activity history and members are notified.
- AC4: Ties go to the option listed first; no votes → "No winner".

## Required tests

Unit for `tally`/`pickWinner`; integration for routes and lazy close with fixed `now` (AC1–AC4); E2E: create, vote, change vote, close.

## Implementation notes (owner fills in)

**Built (Nov 3, Dev + Claude Code):**

- **Service** `src/server/services/polls/service.ts`: `createPoll`, `vote`, `closePoll`, `closeDuePolls`, `listPolls`
  (open first, closing soonest; then closed, most recently closed), plus `canClosePoll` for the page. Pure `results.ts`:
  `tally`, `pickWinner` (most votes; tie → lowest `position`; no votes → null), `closedSummary` (activity text).
- **Routes** exactly as api.md § PB-14, all `teamRoute`: `GET/POST …/polls`, `PUT …/polls/[pollId]/vote`,
  `POST …/polls/[pollId]/close`. The poll must belong to the team, and the option must belong to the poll (404 otherwise).
- **Page** `/teams/[teamId]/polls`: open polls as radio groups (changing the choice changes the vote; results show
  after you vote), a countdown plus the absolute closing time, **Close poll now** for the creator or a leader
  (ConfirmDialog). Closed polls show "Final choice: X" (or "No winner: nobody voted."), with a bar and count text per
  option. "New poll" dialog: question, 2–6 options (add/remove), closing time in the viewer's zone. `loading.tsx`
  (`role="status"`) and `error.tsx`.

**Decisions / gotchas:**

- **Concurrency.** `vote` and `closePoll` lock the poll row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`) in their transaction.
  - A vote can't land after a close.
  - Two lazy closes (two people opening the page after `closesAt`) log `POLL_CLOSED` once and notify once.
    `dedupeKey: poll-closed:<id>` is a backstop.
  - Double clicks and two tabs update the single `(pollId, userId)` vote.
  - Tested with `Promise.allSettled`: concurrent votes; vote vs. early close (5 rounds); 3 concurrent lazy closes.
- **Lazy close** (no cron, ADR 5). `listPolls` and `vote` close expired polls first. A vote arriving after `closesAt`
  closes the poll (committed) and then returns 409 "This poll has closed."
- **Who closed it.** An automatic close is attributed in activity to the poll's creator, with "at its closing time" in
  the summary. The activity feed shows "Someone" for a null actor, which read badly. Early close notifies everyone
  except the closer; an automatic close notifies everyone.
- **No Intl in client render.** The absolute closing time is formatted on the server (`formatWhen` from
  `meetings/format.ts`, parts-based). The countdown (`closesInLabel`) is plain arithmetic, starts from the server's
  `now` so hydration matches, then ticks each minute.
- The closing-time input is converted with `TZDate` in the viewer's zone (unit-tested across the Nov 1 DST change).
- Dialogs are mounted only while open; forms use `noValidate` and show the API's field errors.
- Results are hidden until you vote, so early votes don't sway the rest. The total vote count is always shown.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/polls.test.ts` (9: tally, winner/ties, summary, countdown, close-time conversion),
`tests/integration/polls.test.ts` (16: AC1–AC4, lazy close with a fixed later `now`, concurrency, 403/404, sort order),
