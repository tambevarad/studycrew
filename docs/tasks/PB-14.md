**User story:** As a member, I can create a poll, vote, and see the final choice recorded.

## Files you own

`src/server/services/polls/**`, `src/app/api/v1/teams/[teamId]/polls/**`, `src/app/(app)/teams/[teamId]/polls/**`,
`src/components/features/polls/**`, tests `polls*`.

## Build

- Service: `createPoll` (2–6 unique options, `closesAt` must be in the future → 400; `POLL_CREATED` activity + notify
  others), `vote` (upsert one vote per member; 409 "This poll has closed." after `closesAt`/`closedAt`), `closePoll`
  (creator or leader; early close), `closeDuePolls(teamId, now)` called by list/get to lazily close expired polls,
  setting `closedAt`, `winningOptionId` (most votes; tie → lowest `position`; no votes → null) and logging `POLL_CLOSED`
  with summary "closed the poll “Q” — winner: “A” (3 votes)" + notify `POLL_CLOSED`. `listPolls` (open first, then closed).
- Pure `results.ts`: `tally(options, votes)`, `pickWinner(tally)`.
- Page: open polls with radio options (change vote until close), live counts after voting, closing countdown;
  closed polls show the winner highlighted (`success`) and a bar per option with counts as text. Create dialog with
  dynamic option list (add/remove, 2–6), close date/time.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: A poll needs 2–6 distinct options and a future closing time.
- AC2: Each member has one vote that can change until the poll closes; after closing voting returns 409.
- AC3: When a poll closes (time passed or closed early), the final choice is recorded in activity history and members are notified.
- AC4: Ties go to the option listed first; no votes → "No winner".

## Required tests

Unit for `tally`/`pickWinner`; integration for routes and lazy close with fixed `now` (AC1–AC4); E2E: create, vote, change vote, close.
